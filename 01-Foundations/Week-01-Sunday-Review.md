## 1. Looking back on Week 1, what concept are you most confident about now?

I am most confident about thinking like a SOC analyst. I learned that a SOC analyst does not make assumptions but instead gathers evidence from multiple sources before making a decision. This mindset is the foundation of every security investigation.

## 2. What part of Week 1 do you think will take the most practice?

---

## 3. How has your understanding of cybersecurity changed this week?

Before this week, I mostly viewed cybersecurity as protecting computers from hackers. Now I understand that cybersecurity also involves investigating events, collecting evidence, and understanding how systems behave during normal and abnormal activity.

---

## 4. Explain why evidence is important during an investigation.

Evidence allows a SOC analyst to make informed decisions instead of guessing. Logs, user activity, and system events help determine what actually happened and reduce the chance of making incorrect conclusions.

I am excited to learn more about Windows Event Logs and SIEM platforms because they are essential tools used by SOC analysts during investigations.

# Weekly Investigation Challenge

### Scenario

A user reports that they cannot log into their computer and believes someone attempted to access their account.

### My Investigation Steps

1. Review Windows login events.
2. Look for failed login attempts.
3. Check the time the attempts occurred.
4. Verify whether a successful login followed the failed attempts.
5. Determine whether additional suspicious activity occurred afterward.
