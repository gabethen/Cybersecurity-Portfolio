# Mission 16 - Home Lab Fundamentals

## Objective

Understand what a home cybersecurity lab is, why SOC analysts use one, and prepare my computer for hands-on cybersecurity practice.

## What I Learned

A home cybersecurity lab is a controlled environment where I can safely practice cybersecurity skills without affecting real systems.

A SOC analyst can use a home lab to gain hands-on experience instead of only reading about cybersecurity concepts. It provides an environment where I can generate activity, investigate logs, identify suspicious behavior, and practice responding to security alerts.

A virtual machine is a separate computer environment that runs inside my physical computer. Using a virtual machine allows me to experiment in an isolated environment instead of making changes directly to my everyday computer.

## What I Want to Investigate

Inside my home lab, I want to practice investigating:

* Login attempts
* Windows Event Logs
* Network connections
* Processes
* Suspicious activity
* SIEM alerts

My goal is to gain a better understanding of what to look for during an investigation and how to identify important evidence.

## My Biggest Challenge

My biggest challenge will be not having a physical instructor or someone directly over my shoulder to tell me what I am doing correctly or incorrectly. I may overlook something or make a mistake without realizing it, so I will need structured guidance and verification while building and using the lab.

## Lab Hardware

**Computer:** MacBook Pro 13-inch (2020)
**Processor:** Apple M1
**RAM:** 8 GB
**Available Storage:** 46.1 GB / 245.11 GB

## Lab Plan

I will start with a small and controlled lab environment because my Mac has limited RAM and available storage.

The initial goal is:

**MacBook → Virtual Machine → Windows → Windows Event Logs → Generate Activity → Investigate → Document Findings in GitHub**

As I become more comfortable, I can expand the lab to include additional security monitoring and SIEM tools.

## Key Takeaway

A home lab will allow me to move from learning cybersecurity concepts theoretically to actually generating, observing, and investigating security activity in a controlled environment. My goal is to build practical SOC skills while documenting my progress and investigations in GitHub.
