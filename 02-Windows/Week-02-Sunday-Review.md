# Weekly Investigation Challenge

### Scenario

A SIEM generates an alert showing three failed login attempts followed by one successful login.

### My Investigation Steps

1. Identify the user account involved.
2. Review the timestamps of each login attempt.
3. Determine whether the successful login came from the same device or IP address.
4. Check for additional suspicious activity after the login.
5. Review other security logs to determine whether the account may have been compromised.
